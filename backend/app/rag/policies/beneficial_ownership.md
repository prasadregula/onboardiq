# KYC-POL-003 Beneficial Ownership (FinCEN CDD Rule)

## 1. Requirement
For every legal entity customer the bank must identify and verify each beneficial owner under two prongs before the account is opened (31 CFR 1010.230).

## 2. Ownership prong
Each natural person who directly or indirectly owns 25 percent or more of the equity interests of the legal entity customer. Ownership through intermediate entities is traced through each tier and multiplied: a person holding 50 percent of a holding company that owns 60 percent of the customer holds 30 percent and must be identified. The sum of declared ownership may not exceed 100 percent; a declaration exceeding 100 percent is a data integrity failure and must be corrected before proceeding.

## 3. Control prong
One natural person with significant responsibility to control, manage or direct the legal entity customer, for example a Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Managing Member, General Partner, President or any other individual who regularly performs similar functions. Exactly one control person is required even when no individual meets the ownership prong.

## 4. Ownership coverage
Where the declared ownership interests identified to natural persons total less than 75 percent, the remaining interests must be explained (for example widely held, publicly traded parent, or many small holders) or additional owners must be declared. Unexplained coverage gaps are resolved with the customer through a request for additional information; they are not escalated unless accompanied by a screening or fraud concern.

## 5. Identification of each beneficial owner
Each beneficial owner and the control person must provide the identifying information in KYC-POL-001 Section 2 and an acceptable identification document under KYC-POL-001 Section 3. A beneficial owner without an identification document on file is a missing item to be requested from the customer.

## 6. Exemptions
Excluded from the beneficial ownership requirement: regulated US financial institutions, entities listed on a US stock exchange, US governmental entities, registered investment companies, and sole proprietorships and unincorporated associations (which are treated as natural persons). Trusts are not legal entity customers, but a trustee is treated as the control person.

## 7. Certification
The individual opening the account certifies the accuracy of the beneficial ownership information. Screening under KYC-POL-004 is performed on every beneficial owner and the control person, not only on the entity.
