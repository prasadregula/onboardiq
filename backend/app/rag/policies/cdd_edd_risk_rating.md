# KYC-POL-002 Customer Due Diligence, Risk Rating and Enhanced Due Diligence

## 1. Risk-based approach
Every customer receives a risk rating of Low, Medium or High at onboarding and at each periodic review. The rating determines the level of due diligence, the review frequency (Low: 36 months, Medium: 24 months, High: 12 months) and whether analyst approval is required before account opening.

## 2. Risk factors and weights
The customer risk score (0–100) is the sum of the applicable factor weights below. Factors are additive; the model may increase but never decrease a factor weight.

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---|
| Customer or beneficial owner resident in, or entity formed in, a FATF high-risk jurisdiction | 40 |
| Customer or beneficial owner resident in a jurisdiction under increased monitoring | 20 |
| Politically exposed person (customer, beneficial owner or control person) | 40 |
| Possible sanctions match pending disposition | 30 |
| Adverse media related to financial crime | 25 |
| Cash-intensive business (restaurants, convenience stores, car washes, laundromats, ATM operators) | 20 |
| Money services business, virtual asset service provider, gaming, or arms dealer | 35 |
| Non-resident alien or foreign entity without US operations | 15 |
| Layered ownership through one or more intermediate entities (holding companies), trusts, or nominee shareholders | 20 |
| Anticipated international wire activity or expected monthly volume over 250,000 USD | 15 |
| Private banking or correspondent relationship | 20 |
| Non-profit or charity operating internationally | 15 |
| Document quality issues resolved by the customer at onboarding | 5 |
| Customer is an established US-resident individual with a single low-value deposit product | 0 |

## 3. Rating bands
Score 0–29: Low. Score 30–59: Medium. Score 60 and above: High. A politically exposed person is rated High regardless of score. A customer with an open sanctions alert cannot be rated until the alert is dispositioned.

## 4. Enhanced Due Diligence triggers
Enhanced Due Diligence and analyst approval are mandatory for: every High-risk customer; every politically exposed person and their immediate family members and close associates; correspondent and private banking accounts; any customer with a confirmed or possible sanctions match; any customer whose beneficial ownership cannot be fully established. EDD includes source of wealth and source of funds documentation, senior management approval, and increased transaction monitoring.

## 5. Medium-risk customers
Medium-risk customers may be onboarded after a KYC analyst reviews the risk factors and confirms the rating. Analyst review is documentary and does not require senior management approval unless a factor is upgraded to High.

## 6. Low-risk customers
Low-risk customers with complete, consistent, verified identification and no screening alerts may be approved automatically by the onboarding system. The system records the risk factors and policy sections relied upon.

## 7. Expected activity
The application must capture the purpose of the account, expected monthly transaction volume, and whether international wires are anticipated. Material deviations from expected activity after onboarding are referred to transaction monitoring.
