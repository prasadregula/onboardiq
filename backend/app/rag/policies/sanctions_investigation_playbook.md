# KYC-POL-006 Sanctions Investigation Playbook (BSA Officers)
<!-- access: BsaOfficer, Admin -->

## 1. Purpose and audience
This playbook is restricted to the BSA Officer and delegated BSA staff. It describes how a confirmed or escalated sanctions alert is investigated after the KYC analyst escalates it under KYC-POL-004 Section 4. KYC analysts do not receive this playbook; they escalate and stop.

## 2. Investigation steps
The BSA Officer confirms the identity match using at least two independent identifiers, reviews the original list entry and its program, checks related parties and ownership chains for 50 percent rule exposure, and documents every source consulted. Customer contact about the alert is prohibited while the investigation is open.

## 3. Blocking and reporting decisions
If the match is confirmed and property must be blocked, the BSA Officer blocks it, files the blocked property report with OFAC within 10 business days, and records the decision in the case file. Suspicious activity reporting is decided separately; a suspicious activity report is filed no later than 30 calendar days after initial detection of facts that may constitute a basis for filing.

## 4. Closing an alert as a false positive
A false positive is closed only by the BSA Officer, with the distinguishing identifiers recorded. The disposition is added to the screening regression set so the same name does not re-alert without new information.
